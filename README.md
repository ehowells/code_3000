## Personal Ethics Statement:
Ethics in technology are grounded in the idea that ethical AI is a result of deliberate decision-making that avoids issues such as discriminatory outputs, privacy violations, and other risks that may arise from the use of AI. Explainability, privacy, and bias are important factors in the ethics of AI use, and they are qualitative issues that rely human judgment rather than better datasets. Additionally, ethical responsibility in technology stems from being proactive rather than reactive. It requires identifying potential harms and taking accountability for the outcomes of AI use, and being transparent about how AI is used. I believe that this perspective highlights the importance of integrating ethical considerations into every stage of development, and ethical responsibility comes from moving beyond compliance and instead actively focusing on the social implications of everything I build. 


*This statement is part of the coursework for CSE 3000.*
